## Project Description 
Forge Synesis is a JabRef extension designed to bridge the gap between academic research and active learning. It allows users to transform the content of their research papers and bibliographic entries into structured flashcards, helping them review and retain important information directly within their existing research workflow.

The extension uses the Gemini API content processing to identify relevant concepts, definitions, facts, and key ideas from selected academic documents. These can then be transformed into concise question-and-answer flashcards, reducing the manual effort required to create study material from research papers.

The goal of Forge Synesis is to make knowledge extraction from academic literature faster, more accessible, and more useful for learning. By integrating the flashcard generation process into JabRef, users can move from finding and organizing literature to understanding and reviewing its content without having to switch between multiple applications.

Key Features:
- Generate flashcards from selected academic documents.

- Use AI to identify important concepts and information.

- Create concise question-and-answer cards automatically.

- Integrate flashcard generation directly into the JabRef workflow.

- Reduce the time and effort required to create study material manually.

- Support active recall and repeated review of information extracted from research literature.

Forge Synesis is intended to complement, rather than replace, the user's own learning process: AI assists with creating the material, while the user remains responsible for reviewing, understanding, and validating the generated content.
